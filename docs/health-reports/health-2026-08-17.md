```
Error: EINVAL: invalid argument, readlink
'…\vetpath\.next\package.json'  (errno -4071, syscall: readlink)
```

Next's dev-start routine tries to clean the production `.next` left by `npm run build`, and OneDrive's reparse points make `readlink` fail.
