## The canonical org description (reuse everywhere)

**Long (~100 words):**

> VetPath (vetpathusa.com) is a free planning website for transitioning
> service members, veterans, and their families. A ten-minute
> questionnaire becomes a personal, printable 30/60/90-day plan: which
> benefits to pursue, a career direction with Bureau of Labor Statistics
> pay data, how to fund the training, and a documents checklist. State
> benefits for all 50 states and D.C. are cited to official sources and
> dated. A transition timeline converts a service member's EAS/ETS date
> into real calendar deadlines. VetPath is free for veterans, sells
> nothing to them, and at every claims-related step directs users to
> accredited representatives - never to paid consultants.

**Short (~40 words):**

> Free planning website for transitioning service members, veterans, and
> families: a ten-minute questionnaire becomes a printable 30/60/90-day
> plan covering benefits (cited to official sources, all 50 states),
> career direction with federal pay data, funding options, and deadlines.

**Three facts that differentiate (drop into any "tell us more" box):**
- Every number links to its official source (VA.gov, state agencies,
  BLS/O*NET) with a verified date.
- Claims are always routed to accredited help; the site warns against
  paid claims consultants. Free for veterans is a written rule.
- Privacy: plain-language policy, no advertising trackers, anonymous
  page counting only, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control honored.
