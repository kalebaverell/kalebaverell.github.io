1. The product activates well (44%) on a tiny, warm audience.
2. Traffic exists but is untagged - every future door (Pendleton, OC VSO,
   VRCN newsletter, round 3) has its own QR/tag now, so the next snapshot

   with zero promotion - early word-of-mouth signal.
