VVetPath For Orange County veteran service officers
August 2026

A free planning tool that sends veterans to you better prepared

VetPath is a free website that turns a ten-minute questionnaire into a personal 30/60/90-day gameplan: the benefits worth checking, a career direction, and the documents to gather. It was built with veterans, every number is cited to an official source, and at every claims-related step it points people to one place: accredited help like yours.

What the veterans you serve get

✓
A personal, printable gameplan - not a wall of links. Prioritized 30/60/90-day steps built from their answers.
✓
California benefits cited to CalVet and official sources - the College Fee Waiver, property tax exemption, CalVet home loans, and the new partial state-tax exclusion on military retirement pay. A printable California checklist is available as a companion sheet.
✓
A career direction with real federal pay data, and how to fund the training (GI Bill, VR&E, apprenticeships).
✓
A documents checklist - DD-214, medical records, COE - so their first meeting with you starts further ahead.

What we promise you

✓
We route claims to accredited help, always. The site tells veterans that VSO help is free, and warns against paid claims consultants. We never charge for claims assistance - it is a written rule.
✓
We are not the VA and never determine eligibility - planning and education only, labeled that way throughout.
✓
Free for veterans, always. The tools, the plan, and the printouts cost a veteran nothing - the privacy policy explains how data is handled in plain language.
✓
Costs your office nothing. No contract, no account to manage - just a link or a sheet in the lobby.

What it looks like at your office

  1. Keep this sheet at the counter or in the lobby - the address below is all anyone needs.
  2. A veteran spends about ten minutes on their phone and walks out with a plan they can print.
  3. When the plan says "file a claim," it says to do it with accredited help like yours - and they arrive with their paperwork list started.
