The California veteran benefits checklist

The benefits most California veterans have earned and many never use - each one cited to the official source so you can verify it yourself. Share it, print it, tape it to the fridge.

Federal first - the four to lock in wherever you live

VA health care - medical care, mental health, and prescriptions through the VA. Enroll even if you have other coverage.
Verify: va.gov/health-care
VA disability compensation - tax-free monthly pay for service-connected conditions. Accredited VSO help (VFW, American Legion, DAV, county service officers) is free - never pay for basic claims assistance.
Verify: va.gov/disability
GI Bill & education benefits - tuition, a housing allowance, and training support for you or, in some cases, your dependents.
Verify: va.gov/education
VA home loan - no down payment, no PMI. Start by requesting your Certificate of Eligibility.
Verify: va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans

California state benefits - via CalVet and partner agencies

Military retirement pay is no longer fully taxed - this is new - for tax years 2025 through 2029 California excludes up to $20,000 of federal military retirement pay and Survivor Benefit Plan annuities from state income tax. Federal AGI must be at or below $125,000 individually, or $250,000 joint or surviving spouse. California was the last state to fully tax military pensions, so this one is easy to miss.
Verify: ftb.ca.gov - Publication 1032, or R&TC sections 17132.9 and 17132.10
College Tuition Fee Waiver for dependents - waives system-wide tuition and fees at California Community Colleges, CSU, and UC for eligible children, spouses, and surviving spouses of service-connected disabled or deceased veterans. Four plans (A-D) with different eligibility - apply through your County Veterans Service Office.
Verify: calvet.ca.gov/VetServices/Pages/College-Fee-Waiver.aspx
Disabled Veterans' Property Tax Exemption - reduces the assessed value of your principal residence if you are rated 100 percent service-connected or unemployable, with a larger exemption for lower-income households. Amounts adjust annually - your county assessor applies it.
Verify: calvet.ca.gov (state benefits) and your county assessor's office
CalVet Home Loans - a state-run loan program separate from the federal VA loan - up to 100 percent financing, no private mortgage insurance, and manual underwriting that looks at the whole picture. California homes only.
Verify: calvet.ca.gov/HomeLoans
Veterans Homes of California - a statewide network of homes providing affordable long-term care at several levels for aged and disabled veterans and their eligible spouses or domestic partners.
Verify: calvet.ca.gov (Veterans Homes)
Recreation - reduced-fee licenses and the Distinguished Veteran Pass - eligible disabled veterans can get reduced-fee hunting and fishing licenses, plus a pass for free day-use and camping at participating state parks.
Verify: calvet.ca.gov (state benefits) and parks.ca.gov
Start with your County Veterans Service Office. Every California county has one, and their help filing claims and applying for state benefits is free - they are the people who process the College Fee Waiver and know your county's property tax process. CalVet lists all 58 offices. Never pay for basic claims assistance.
