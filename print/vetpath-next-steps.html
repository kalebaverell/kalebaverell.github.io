4Decision two: how it pays for itself

The rule I would hold to: free for veterans, always. The question is who does pay.

RouteWhat it means, and the honest catch
Organizations payVSOs, universities, and employers license it for the veterans they serve. Closest to the mission, slowest to close.
Grants and sponsorsVeteran-focused foundations and corporate giving. Real money, but it needs a nonprofit arm or a fiscal sponsor to receive most of it.
Entity structureThis is the fork underneath the other two. Nonprofit opens grants and closes investment. For-profit is the reverse. We should decide the direction Saturday even if we file later.
