4Decision two: the money - the playbook is written

The nine-page funding playbook covers every option with published numbers: state veteran-assistance grants ($200-300k awards in Texas), veteran foundations (~$100k average, but they require two years of financial history), DoD SBIR contracts ($75k-250k entry, for-profit only), organization licensing, and the revenue we refuse on trust grounds.

The playbook's five closing questions walk the decision; everything else follows from the answer in one step.
