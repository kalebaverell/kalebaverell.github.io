Why trust us

Built to be checked.

Skeptical is smart. Here are the rules this platform operates by - and every one of them is verifiable.

We never determine eligibility

Only the VA, your state, or an accredited VSO can. Every card links to the official source to confirm.

Free accredited help first

Claims guidance always points to free accredited representatives - never to paid consultants.

Your data stays yours

Browse anonymously and everything stays in your browser. Saved plans are encrypted and visible only to you.

Dates on everything

Verified content carries the date we checked it. Stale dates are treated as bugs, not business as usual.
