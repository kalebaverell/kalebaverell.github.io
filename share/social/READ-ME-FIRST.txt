VETPATH FACEBOOK LAUNCH - READ ME FIRST
========================================

Frank - everything you need is in this folder. Ten minutes start to
finish. One thing before anything posts:

YOUR SIGN-OFF (required)
The 8 slides speak in YOUR voice - "The day I left the military...",
"I found out the hard way", "my partner and I built it ourselves",
"don't learn it the hard way like I did". Read them. If any line is
not how you'd say it, tell Kaleb the exact wording you want and the
slide gets fixed in minutes. If you want your service branch named on
slide 1, or a real photo of you on slide 1 or 5, send it - both make
this hit harder.

HOW TO POST (Facebook)
1. Start a new post on your personal profile.
2. Add ALL 8 photos in one post, in filename order (01 first - it
   becomes the big tile in the feed preview).
3. Paste everything in CAPTION.txt as the post text. The link stays
   in - do not remove it, it is how we count who came from Facebook.
4. Post it. Do not edit the post for at least an hour afterward.

WHEN
Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday, 9-11 AM your time. Peak windows pull
about 3x the reach of off-hours.

THE FIRST 90 MINUTES DECIDE EVERYTHING
Facebook watches early engagement, then decides how far to spread it.
- Tip off 3-5 friends/family the day before: "posting Tuesday morning,
  would love a comment."
- Reply to EVERY comment, same hour if you can. Your replies count
  double, and you are the credible face of this.
- The caption ends with a question to veterans on purpose - every
  answer in the comments pushes the post further.

AFTER THE POST HAS MOMENTUM: GROUPS
This is where the real audience is - unit and base groups, veteran
groups, South OC community groups, military-spouse groups. Share the
post into groups YOU are genuinely a member of:
- Read each group's promo rules first. Some need admin approval.
- Two personal sentences + the share beats a copy-paste blast.
- One or two groups a day. Never groups you just joined for this.
- If an admin objects: apologize, move on.

RULES THAT PROTECT US
- Never tag Camp Pendleton, the Marine Corps, the VA, or any
  government page. We never imply endorsement.
- Everything on the slides is a product fact - no invented numbers.

NEXT TOUCH (3-5 days later)
A 45-60 second selfie video of you talking - phone at eye level, good
light, captions on. Script skeleton is with Kaleb; your own words beat
ours. It posts as its own follow-up.
