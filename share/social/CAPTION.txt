The day I left the military, I had no idea what came next - or which benefits were even mine. My partner and I built something so the next generation doesn't have that experience.

VetPath is a free website that turns ten minutes of questions into a real plan for life after the military - the benefits you've earned (every number linked to its official source), a career direction, and your deadlines on an actual calendar.

Free for veterans, always. Nothing sold, nothing tracked, and claims always point to free accredited help.
