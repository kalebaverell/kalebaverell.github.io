THE VETPATH FOLLOW-UP VIDEO - YOUR SCRIPT
==========================================

One take, 45-60 seconds, you talking to one veteran. Everything in
[brackets] is yours to fill with your own words - your words beat
anything we write, every time. If a line doesn't sound like you,
change it until it does.

THE SCRIPT (five beats, ~50 seconds)
------------------------------------

1. WHO YOU ARE (about 8 seconds)
   "I'm [first name]. I [one line about your service - branch and
   roughly how long]."

2. THE HONEST MOMENT (about 10 seconds) - this is the whole video
   "When I left the military, [the one thing you wish someone had
   told you]."

3. WHAT WE BUILT (about 12 seconds)
   "That's why my partner and I built VetPath - a free website that
   turns ten minutes of questions into a real plan: your benefits,
   your deadlines, a career direction - every number linked to the
   official source."

4. THE PROMISE (about 8 seconds)
   "It's free for veterans. It always will be. And we don't sell
   anything to you."

5. THE ASK (about 10 seconds)
   "It's at vetpathusa.com - link in the post. If you know somebody
   leaving the military this year, send this to them."

PROMPTS FOR BEAT 2 (pick the one that's true for you)
-----------------------------------------------------
- The benefit you found out about too late
- The deadline nobody mentioned until it had passed
- The feeling of the first month out - what you didn't expect
- Something you watched another veteran miss that still bothers you

WHAT NOT TO SAY (the guardrails)
--------------------------------
- Nothing that sounds like the Marine Corps, the VA, or the
  government endorses us. You speaking as a veteran: perfect.
- No numbers about users or money - just what the site does.
- Don't promise outcomes ("you'll get your rating") - VetPath plans,
  officials decide.
