FRANK'S VIDEO KIT - READ THIS FIRST
====================================

This is touch two. The carousel introduced the story; this video puts
your face and voice on it. Research and our own playbook agree: a
45-60 second selfie video of a real veteran talking beats anything
produced, polished, or edited. Authentic wins.

WHEN
----
3 to 5 days after the carousel posts, same window: Tuesday, Wednesday,
or Thursday, 9-11 AM your time. Two touches beat one, and the gap
keeps the algorithm treating them as separate stories.

HOW TO FILM (five rules, nothing else matters)
----------------------------------------------
1. Phone VERTICAL (portrait). One video works everywhere that way.
2. Camera at eye level - stack some books, don't look down at it.
3. Face a window, or step outside. Daylight on your face, not behind.
4. Quiet room, phone within arm's reach so your voice is close.
5. One take, conversational, like you're telling one buddy. Your
   first or second take is almost always the keeper. Do NOT polish.

The script and a big-print cue card are in this kit. Read it twice,
then put it down and say it your way. 45-60 seconds. If you run long,
cut beat 3 detail, never beat 2 - the honest moment IS the video.

Keep it civilian: no uniform, not on base, no official logos in
frame. You speak as a veteran, never for the Corps or the VA.

HOW TO POST - FACEBOOK
----------------------
1. Post it as a REEL (vertical video = Reel = the most reach FB gives).
2. Facebook auto-captions reels - check the captions for errors before
   posting (most people watch muted).
3. Use the caption in VIDEO-CAPTIONS.txt, link included.
4. Same first-hour rules as the carousel: don't edit the post, reply
   to every comment, Kaleb comments within 15 minutes.

HOW TO POST - LINKEDIN
----------------------
1. Upload the SAME video as a native LinkedIn post (upload the file -
   never paste a link to the Facebook post).
2. Turn on captions when LinkedIn offers them.
3. Caption from VIDEO-CAPTIONS.txt - NO link in the post body.
4. Immediately add the first comment with the link (it's written out
   for you in VIDEO-CAPTIONS.txt).

AFTER IT'S UP
-------------
Same playbook as the carousel: after it gets some traction on your
profile, share it into the veteran groups you're genuinely part of,
one or two a day, as your story - not an ad.
