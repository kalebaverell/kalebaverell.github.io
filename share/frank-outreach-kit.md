## 1. Email to Camp Pendleton Transition Readiness Program

**Subject:** Free planning resource for Marines approaching EAS - resource table question
**Attach:** vetpath-pendleton-onepager.pdf, vetpath-california-checklist.pdf

Good morning,

My name is [Frank's name], [one line: service background + South Orange
County resident]. I'm writing to ask what it takes to get a one-page
resource sheet in front of Marines going through the Transition Readiness
Program.

The resource is VetPath (vetpathusa.com), a free planning website my
partner and I built with veterans. A Marine spends about ten minutes
answering questions and walks out with a printable 30/60/90-day plan:
benefits worth checking, a career direction with real pay data, and a
documents checklist. It speaks EAS natively - enter the date and every
phase carries real calendar months. Every number links to an official
source, and at every claims-related step it points Marines to accredited
help - never to paid consultants.

To be direct about what it is not: it is free for Marines with nothing
sold to them, it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of
War, the Marine Corps, or the VA, and we are not asking for any
endorsement - just whether a resource sheet like the attached can sit on
the resource table or go in the transition packet, and what your review
process requires.

If it is easier to judge in person, I am local and happy to give a
five-minute walkthrough at your convenience - or simply try it yourself
at vetpathusa.com first.

Thank you for what you do for transitioning Marines.

## 2. Phone script - OC Veterans Service Office (714-480-6555)

**Goal of the call:** three answers, one offer. Keep it under five minutes.

Opening:
"Good morning - my name is [Frank], I'm a veteran here in [city]. I have
three quick questions and one thing to offer, if you can point me to the
right person."

Ask, in order:
1. "Which Community Veterans Engagement Board covers Orange County, and
   how does someone join or attend?" (If none exists: "Who convenes the
   veteran-serving organizations around here?")
2. "Who runs the county's Veterans Employment Program, and how do
   outside veteran-serving organizations plug into it?"
3. "If a free planning website wanted to leave printed resource sheets
   in your lobby - free for veterans, points all claims to accredited
   help like your office - what's the process for that?"

The offer:
"We built a free tool called VetPath - it turns ten minutes of questions
into a printable benefits-and-career plan, cited to official sources,
and it tells every veteran that county service officer help is free.
I'd like to drop off a one-page sheet about it and a California benefits
checklist - who should I leave those with?"

Wrap: get a name and an email for follow-up, send the two PDFs same day.
